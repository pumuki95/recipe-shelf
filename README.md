# The Recipe Shelf

My recipe collection, built from YouTube and Instagram cooking videos.

Live site: https://pumuki95.github.io/recipe-shelf/

Each recipe has a photo of the finished dish, servings, cook time, nutrition, tags, a scalable ingredient list and step-by-step instructions. Recipes added to the grocery list are combined and sorted by aisle.

- **Plan**: put recipes on days of the week, send the whole week to the grocery list, and share the plan or list (share sheet, WhatsApp, or a link that loads the plan on another phone).
- **Compare**: groups of similar recipes (e.g. all cinnamon rolls) ranked by goal: protein per calorie, fewest calories, least (added) sugar, most fiber, lowest net carbs, quickest, fewest ingredients.
- **Light / dark**: follows the device by default; the button in the top bar switches it.

## How it's organised

- `index.html` – the site
- `recipes.json` – all recipes, generated by `python3 build.py`
- `data/` – one file per video with its recipes (`_playlist-order.txt` sets the order, newest first; `_families.json` lists the comparison groups — add new recipes to a group when they fit)
- `img/` – a photo of each finished dish, taken from the video
- `edits.json` – changes made with "Edit recipe" on the site (applied on top of `recipes.json`; never overwritten by `build.py`)
- `tools/` – the frame scanner used to pick cover photos, the editor source, and `nutrition.py`

## Fiber and sugars

`build.py` fills in fiber, total sugars, added sugars and sugar alcohols for every recipe from its ingredient list (`tools/nutrition.py`, typical per-100 g label values). Those numbers show as "est." on the site. Any value a creator states is written into the recipe's `macros` and listed in `stated`, and is never overwritten.

## Cover photos

For each video, `tools/scan.js` samples a frame about every 2 seconds and scores it for sharpness, exposure and colour. The top 12 are reviewed and the cover is the one where the food is fully plated, well lit, sharp and not blocked by hands, utensils or on-screen text.
